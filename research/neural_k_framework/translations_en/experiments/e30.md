## Experiment overview {#overview}

## Purpose

Measure how explicit L2 changes the static loss landscape over which the same set of
functions competes, providing one static contribution to grokking-style rule selection.
This experiment does not independently adjudicate the dynamical causes of real
grokking or establish that this contribution is the only cause. Grokking without
weight decay requires separate real-training evidence, such as
[E31's no-WD Transformer training](../../experiments/experiment_31_loss_amplitude_grokking/POWER_MOD97_ALIGNMENT_REPORT.md).

## Why this experiment was run, and how it was judged {#motivation}

## 1. Question

[The original grokking experiments by Power et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177)
showed that weight decay markedly promotes delayed generalization, but grokking and
rule recovery do not take weight decay as a necessary condition.
[Zhang et al. (NeurIPS 2025)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/92f67b9047fa7a43d7506054b5f0ec6a-Paper-Conference.pdf)
also found high-norm generalizing solutions with WanD without explicit regularization.
A common explanation still treats weight decay as a special intervention in the
optimizer dynamics;
but for explicit L2, the optimizer in fact only receives the gradient of the full
scalar objective

$$
J_\lambda(\theta)
=
L_D(\theta)+\lambda\lVert\theta\rVert^2/2
$$

The two most basic objects of the original framework are precisely the loss landscape
and the optimizer; explicit L2 merely adds a term to the same scalar loss and is not a
new theoretical mechanism. The preferred prediction should be: the raw-loss landscape
without L2 can already support rule takeover, and adding L2 strengthens this
preference by changing the static landscape.

## 2. Competing explanations

1. **Full static-landscape explanation.** At similar raw BCE, increasing $\lambda$
   increases the static mass of correct AND continuations and compresses the function
   diversity of incorrect continuations.
2. **Fit-depth-only explanation.** The L2 conditions have more AND only because their
   raw BCE is lower; after matched-BCE the difference should vanish.
3. **optimizer-specific explanation.** The static landscape is nearly unchanged, and
   real training differences come mainly from the path.

E30 directly adjudicates only the first two. The third requires independent dynamical
controls; a change in the static landscape does not rule out optimizer paths also
affecting real-training selection and delay.

## 3. Fixed task and measure

- Input: all 8-bit strings; the target is `x0 AND x1`; the remaining 6 bits are nuisance.
- Training set: 10 samples from each of the four main semantic cells, 40 frozen samples in total.
- Network: `8->16->16->1` tanh, 433 standardized Gaussian coordinates.
- Reference measure: coordinate-wise independent $N(0,1)$; the network forward pass uses fan-in scaling.
- Static sampling: direct constrained SMC; 16 replicas, 8192 particles each.
- Function audit: 2048 parameters saved per replica, with the hard function decided on all 256 points.

## 4. Staged threshold freezing

This experiment is not a fully blind single-step preregistration, but a staged
calibration based only on BCE, norms, and sampling diagnostics. Thresholds must not be
adjusted based on the AND mass to be reported.

The final three main conditions are:

| $\lambda$ | Static constraint | Actual raw BCE mean |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0 | $L_D\le0.00268$ | 0.00258150 |
| $5\times10^{-5}$ | $J_\lambda\le0.0160$ | 0.00251046 |
| $10^{-4}$ | $J_\lambda\le0.0211$ | 0.00258978 |

The middle point's BCE is about 3% lower than the two endpoints, so the conditions can
only be called approximately matched-BCE. All figures and tables must also show the
actual BCE; the three points must not be written as exactly equal.

## 5. Quality gates

- replica log-volume standard deviation no higher than 1 nat;
- maximum probe cross-replica standard deviation no higher than 0.03;
- replica standard deviation of the full AND mass no higher than 0.05;
- all saved samples satisfy the constraint and 100% hard-fit the training set;
- use the replica, not the particle, as the paired statistical unit.

Floating-point boundary checks use a relative tolerance plus 16 float32 ULPs; the
minimum tolerance for deep losses remains $10^{-12}$.

## 6. External connection

[Zhang et al. (NeurIPS 2025)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/92f67b9047fa7a43d7506054b5f0ec6a-Paper-Conference.pdf)
used the Wang--Landau method to observe a high-entropy advantage of low-loss
generalizing states in a modular-arithmetic Transformer.
E30 does not reproduce their full Transformer entropy map; instead, in a rigorously
auditable 433-parameter system, it directly compares the function-level static mass of
the explicit-L2 full objective.

## Results, interpretation, and limits {#results}

## 1. Three-point main result

The raw BCE of the three conditions differs by at most about 3.1%, and all saved
networks 100% hard-fit the training set:

| $\lambda$ | raw BCE mean | full AND mass | replica range | number of full functions | half norm mean |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.00258150 | 53.683% | 50.391%--57.178% | 4190 | 369.70 |
| $5\times10^{-5}$ | 0.00251046 | 58.423% | 50.830%--66.406% | 1246 | 268.76 |
| $10^{-4}$ | 0.00258978 | 73.318% | 66.016%--78.125% | 86 | 184.97 |

The full AND mass increases monotonically with the L2 coefficient, while the
full-function diversity drops from 4190 to 1246 and then to 86.

## 2. Replica-level statistics

```text
lambda 0 -> 5e-5:
  AND +4.739 percentage points
  13/16 replicas in the same direction
  bootstrap 95% CI: 2.823--6.555 percentage points
  paired t-test p=2.35e-4
  Wilcoxon p=1.92e-3

lambda 5e-5 -> 1e-4:
  AND +14.896 percentage points
  16/16 replicas in the same direction
  bootstrap 95% CI: 12.097--17.502 percentage points
  paired t-test p=2.80e-8
  Wilcoxon p=4.37e-4

lambda 0 -> 1e-4:
  AND +19.635 percentage points
  16/16 replicas in the same direction
  bootstrap 95% CI: 17.508--21.570 percentage points
```

The middle point's raw BCE is slightly lower, which may slightly inflate its AND mass;
but the BCE of $\lambda=0$ and $10^{-4}$ differ by only 0.32%, so the main endpoint
difference is not affected by this boundary.

## 4. Conceptual verdict

With training fit depth approximately matched, explicit L2 still systematically
increases the static mass of correct AND continuations and compresses the full-function
diversity. What is directly supported is therefore one static selection contribution:

> Explicit L2 reshuffles the parameter mass of training-set-consistent continuations
> by changing the low-$J$ geometry of the full scalar objective. This static effect is
> observable without running an optimizer, but it cannot thereby be identified as the
> sole dynamical cause of real grokking.

The no-WD condition by itself already gives AND about 53.7% static mass, showing that
the rule can become the dominant candidate in a pure raw-loss landscape; this does
not itself measure delayed generalization.
[E31's no-WD Transformer training](../../experiments/experiment_31_loss_amplitude_grokking/POWER_MOD97_ALIGNMENT_REPORT.md)
provides separate real-training evidence of grokking without weight decay. Explicit
L2 strengthens an existing static preference here, but its contributions to training
paths, reachability, and grokking time were not independently separated.

This result does not add a new theoretical element; it clarifies the direct use of the
original framework: the most basic objects remain the loss landscape and the
optimizer. Any term genuinely written into the scalar loss, whether called a
regularizer, an auxiliary loss, or anything else, merely co-shapes the same landscape
and has no special theoretical status. When analyzing such interventions, first
measure the corresponding static function landscape, then use independent dynamical
controls to assess optimizer contributions; static measurements cannot replace that
causal separation.

## 5. Why this is not "AND already had a lower total norm"

In the fixed cross-section with no WD and `BCE<=0.01`, the ranking of total L2 norm
between AND and other continuations is nearly random: the loss-stratified low-norm AUC
median is 0.5088. The role of L2 is not to read a pre-existing obvious norm label,
but to let `BCE+lambda*R` jointly define different BCE--norm Pareto subsets.

## 6. Deeper-layer and stronger-coefficient boundaries

- At the deeper layer with $\lambda=10^{-4}$ and $J\le0.0186$, all low-$J$ full
  functions found by the 16 far-apart replicas are AND; but the absolute logV standard
  deviation is 5.66 nat and the genealogies are severely degenerate, so we only write
  "all observed modes are AND" and do not certify an exact 100%.
- For $\lambda=2\times10^{-4}$ at $J\le0.0396$, BCE is 0.00535 and AND is 73.42%;
  going further down to $J\le0.03545$, BCE is 0.00470 and AND is 90.79%, but the logV
  standard deviation rises to 1.48 nat. Neither point is a matched-BCE main dose, so
  neither enters the main curve.

## 7. What can and cannot be claimed

What can be claimed:

1. Under this task, architecture, Gaussian measure, and approximately matched-BCE
   protocol, increasing the explicit L2 coefficient increases the static
   full-function mass of AND;
2. the raw-loss landscape without weight decay already supports static rule dominance;
   grokking without WD is supported by the separately cited real-training experiment,
   not directly by this static experiment;
3. the static mass enhancement under explicit L2 shows that landscape changes can
   contribute to rule selection, but is not a unique causal explanation of real
   training or grokking delay.

What cannot be claimed:

1. that the same mechanism has already been directly demonstrated on the original
   Mod97 Transformer;
2. that decoupled AdamW is strictly equal to the explicit-L2 scalar objective;
3. that the AND mass is monotonic in arbitrary $\lambda$, or that
   $\lambda=2\times10^{-4}$ has completed matched-BCE;
4. that the deep-layer static AND probability has been strictly certified as 100%;
5. that the static AND mass difference uniquely explains real grokking dynamics or
   rules out an effect of optimizer paths.
