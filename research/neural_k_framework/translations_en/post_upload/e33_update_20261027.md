# 2026-10-27 Supplement: Independent Complexity Calibration, Logic Certificates, and Local Reversals

This supplement incorporates external logic synthesis and reanalysis of the old 3-bit data completed on 2026-10-24 into E33, with publication preparation dated 2026-10-27. It does not replace the earlier cross-architecture report. This is retrospective calibration against known neural data, not a new blind test or a formal preregistration written after the fact. No new neural training, SMC, or full certificate synthesis was performed for this release.

## 1. Motivation: An Independent Scale Beyond Shared Ordering

A shared ordering across architectures does not by itself establish that the ordering measures complexity. Defining complexity through neural-volume contraction and then stating that complex rules contract faster can still be circular. An independent logical language, cost, and target set must be fixed, and provable minimum implementation costs, external proxies, and Neural K compared while retaining unexpected associations and reversals.

Strictly more logic cannot mean merely a longer expression: redundancy and algebraic cancellation must be excluded, and the claim must refer to an explicit gate library, encoding, and minimum implementation cost. Strict ordering in a fixed gate library must not be upgraded to a universal theorem for arbitrary universal description languages, network architectures, reference measures, or precision protocols. Shared ordering, rule-family biases, and local architectural differences are reported separately here, without requiring every external proxy to preserve the order of every function pair.

## 2. Protocol and Data Scope

### 2.1 Historical Neural Panel and External Proxies

The old 3-bit world contains 256 functions, including 70 balanced functions. Input-variable permutations and overall output complementation define 40 orbits, of which 10 are balanced. This is not NPN classification allowing input-bit complementation. The four architectures are relu_w16_d1, tanh_w16_d1, tanh_w16_d2, and tanh_w32_d1, with 0/1 inputs. Low-loss measurements used 8 pools per target and architecture, 4096 particles per pool, and 16 complete-truth-table BCE thresholds from 0.7 to 0.001. This release only reads completed data and does not recertify global mixing of the old SMC runs.

The old association table already covered 19 proxies, including LZ, SOP operation counts, C1+C2, output entropy, CSR, exact formula trees in two gate libraries, exact decision trees, ANF, CTM, and Walsh/Fourier statistics. Formula trees may repeat subexpressions but are not minimum DAG circuits that share subcomputations. Total influence was directly recomputed for all 256 functions. Walsh mean degree equals total influence numerically and must not be counted as independent evidence. Output entropy is constantly 1 for balanced functions and cannot rank them internally.

The primary comparisons use K(0.001) and two finite-window contraction rates:

\[
s_{\epsilon_1\to\epsilon_2}(h)=
\frac{K_h(\epsilon_2)-K_h(\epsilon_1)}{\log_2(\epsilon_1/\epsilon_2)},\qquad
(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)\in\{(0.01,0.001),(0.0025,0.001)\}.
\]

These finite windows are explicit choices in the retrospective analysis, not preregistered predictions on unseen data or asymptotic slopes. The main counts use 10 balanced orbits. The 70 symmetry-related functions or four highly correlated architectures must not be presented as hundreds of independent rule confirmations.

### 2.2 Explicit Gate Libraries, Certificates, and Timeout Bounds

| Gate library | Allowed gates and costs | Input-complement convention |
|---|---|---|
| aon_paid | AND2, OR2, NOT1 each cost 1 gate | Input NOT is charged |
| aonx_paid | AND2, OR2, NOT1, XOR2 each cost 1 gate | Input NOT is charged |
| aon_free_inputs | AND2, OR2, NOT1 each cost 1 gate | Complemented original-input literals are free |

All three languages allow free original inputs, 0/1 constants, and output complementation. Circuits are acyclic, allow DAG sharing and unrestricted fan-out, and have gate fan-in at most 2. Projections and constants can cost 0 gates. Each budget encodes at most r gates, with arbitrary padding of unused gates. The library with cheap XOR was retained in advance; it cannot be dropped because its correlation is negative, nor may only the most favorable library be reported.

The targets comprise all 10 balanced-orbit representatives from the old panel, 8 fixed single-output 4-bit rules, and 2 dual-output 4-bit tasks: 20 targets times three gate libraries, giving 60 cases. Each case is solved from 0 gates upward, with a cap of 10 gates, at most 30 seconds per instance, and a total scheduling limit of 1200 seconds. Timeouts or unsupported proofs are recorded as unknown with available lower and upper bounds, not as infinite complexity. Fixed targets are generated by [run_synthesis.py](proxy_complexity_20261027/run_synthesis.py), without reading Neural K to select rules.

The original solver was CaDiCaL 1.9.5 with `--plain --no-binary --lrat`. A case is exact only when the current-budget SAT witness is correct on every input and the positive-hint UNSAT LRAT proofs for all smaller budgets pass an independent Python checker. Unsupported negative RAT hints cause certification to be rejected. The checker is not a formally verified proof kernel; the correspondence between CNF and logical semantics is additionally supported by gate truth-table tests, witnesses, and independent small-world enumeration.

### 4.2 Reversals Remain After Matching Some Geometry

After matching output proportion, number of essential variables, and total influence, the 10 balanced orbits provide 5 function pairs with strictly different AON formula costs. The table reports the median pool K of the higher-formula-cost function minus that of the lower-cost function, in bits. All 20 architecture/pair median differences are negative. These local reversals are retained despite positive overall correlation.

| Lower-cost function → higher-cost function | relu_w16_d1 | tanh_w16_d1 | tanh_w16_d2 | tanh_w32_d1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0x17 → 0x2B | -61.507 | -69.557 | -29.897 | -99.098 |
| 0x1B → 0x2B | -175.341 | -340.531 | -135.951 | -459.072 |
| 0x2E → 0x2B | -172.422 | -344.699 | -136.857 | -465.784 |
| 0x1E → 0x2D | -4.786 | -5.334 | -2.165 | -11.331 |
| 0x6A → 0x2D | -100.941 | -66.054 | -13.770 | -76.923 |

All cross-pool differences are negative in 19 architecture/pair cases. The exception is 0x1E→0x2D under tanh_w16_d1, whose cross-pool range is [-11.221,0.005] bits. Each target contributes 8 pools. These ranges are not confidence intervals and do not cover systematic bias shared across pools. The original exploratory follow-up resampled pools 5000 times while retaining cross-loss dependence, without multiple-testing control. The lightweight release recomputation only recalculates endpoint median differences and cross-pool ranges; it does not claim to repeat that bootstrap.

A concrete example is 0x17 (NOT majority3) and 0x2B, obtained by complementing its third input bit. Both are balanced, depend on 3 variables, and have total influence=1.5. Their output-complement-minimized AON formula costs are 4 and 5, whereas tanh_w16_d1 K(0.001) values are 360.716629 and 291.159592 bits. Matching these few metrics does not control every confounder.

With 0/1 encoding, complementing an input bit in the first-layer expression `b + sum_j w_j x_j` requires:

\[
w'_j=-w_j,\qquad b'=b+w_j.
\]

This transformation has absolute Jacobian determinant 1 but generally does not preserve an independent parameter prior. If the original b and w_j are independent and zero-mean, then `Cov(w'_j,b')=-Var(w_j)`. Thus a logical transformation that merely negates one input need not have equal Neural K under the original 0/1 iid prior. Input-NOT charging, input encoding, and the reference measure must be distinguished. This does not invalidate all relationships with description complexity, nor justify deleting this rule pair after the fact.

## 5. Supported Conclusions and Open Questions

External calibration is not absent: fixed gate libraries supply independent minimality evidence, the historical neural panel has stable shared ordering, and some proxies show clearly positive associations. Yet the cheap-XOR library has negative association, and stable local reversals remain across architectures after matching some geometry. All three observations must be retained, without selecting only the highest correlation or relabeling every finite proxy as Kolmogorov complexity.

The joint output distribution is already foundational in the framework. The remaining task is to refine comparison sections, precision coordinates, and the complexity definition, not to reinvent joint outputs as a remedy. Shared ordering, common family biases, and architecture-specific biases must be distinguished within explicit target populations and weights. Claims that a family represents only a few exceptions require a defined population and independent confirmation of expected directions on a subsequent target panel.

Logic-proof checking cannot certify unbiasedness of the old SMC estimates; descriptive correlation on a finite panel cannot prove order preservation for arbitrary networks; and shared-DAG savings cannot replace multi-output neural measurements. Geometric matching and family-sensitivity analyses on existing data remain exploratory and do not become formally preregistered confirmations through a retrospective check of between-pool variation.

Minimal reproduction code is in `proxy_complexity_20261027/`, with dependencies in [requirements.txt](proxy_complexity_20261027/requirements.txt). The minimal bundled data comprise a 256-row function/proxy panel, a 3072-row aggregate K table for all functions across four architectures at 0.01, 0.0025, and 0.001, 304 endpoint proxy-association rows, 60 historical gate-count/bound summaries, and 320 endpoint pool records for the 10 balanced orbits. These suffice for self-contained recomputation of the aggregate comparisons in this supplement, but do not include old neural particles, weights, complete training processes, or the original certificate collection. An excerpt of historical exact labels is not a fresh proof.

From the original experiment directory, the following commands perform only lightweight logic tests, existing-data recomputation, and website figure generation, without training, SMC, or SAT solving:
