E20: Measure Closure between Full Rules, Fixed Datasets, and Margin Cores

Establish a strict bridge between full-target volume and candidate-function mass under a fixed partial training set.

Scripts
experiment_and_n10_candidate_full_rule_smc.pyexperiment_and_n10_hard_margin_bridge_smc.pyexperiment_constant_leave_one_out_smc_consistency.py

Experiment overview

Purpose

E20 tests the strict relationship between the full-rule self-loss volume and the fixed-D candidate mass on a fixed partial training set, avoiding the intuitive interchange of different measurement objects. It consists of three steps: constant leave-one-out, AND four-candidate full-rule, and the hard-cell margin bridge.

Why this experiment was run, and how it was judged

1. Two different objects

Full-target volume:

$$ V_g^{full}(\epsilon)=\mu\{L_{full,g}\le\epsilon\}. $$

Fixed-D candidate mass under a fixed partial training set:

$$ \Omega_D(g;\delta)=\mu\{L_D\le\delta,h_\theta=g\}. $$

The two are related but not equal, and cannot be used to prove each other without bridging.

2. Leave-one-out identity

For 15 constant-0 training points with the held-out state 0110, compare the full constant-0 rule against the single-exception rule at that point. Let:

$$ A_\delta=\{L_{15}\le\delta\},\quad B_{y,\epsilon}=\{L_{16,y}\le\epsilon\},\quad \delta=16\epsilon/15. $$

Because BCE is non-negative, B subset A, and strictly:

$$ \mu(B)=\mu(A)P(B\mid A). $$

The volume ratio of two independent full SMC runs must equal the cross-event ratio of the subset particles.

3. AND four candidates and the margin bridge

For the balanced AND n=10 candidates D440/F040/D040/F440, repeat: delta=16epsilon/10. Independently run one subset SMC and four full-rule SMC runs; then branch from the same subset parent by hard ID, and while keeping L10<=0.065 and the hard cell unchanged, only tighten the maximum BCE margin threshold tau on the 6 heldout points.

tau=ln2 is the hard-cell boundary, and the normalized masses of the four branches must reproduce the parent hard posterior; all subsequent change comes only from the continuous contraction of the margin core inside the same hard function.

4. Boundaries

A zero cross-event count can only give a finite-particle upper bound; a branch tau stopping its descent means the current sampling configuration has reached an attainable lower bound, not a mathematical zero volume. Hard odds only look at the sign and do not equal the full soft-margin event volume.

Results, interpretation, and limits

1. Three-way closure of constant leave-one-out

At the 7 thresholds where both full events are measurable, the independent full-rule volume ratio closes with the subset conditional cross-event ratio, with a maximum natural-log ratio residual of about 0.071. Hard sign odds can differ significantly from the full-rule volume ratio, but once the soft-margin conditional probability is added back, the identity holds.

2. Closure of the AND four candidates

For the four candidates, the independent full-rule SMC runs and the subset cross events show a maximum closure residual of about 0.049 in the jointly measurable region. This confirms that full-rule and fixed-D are not the same quantity, yet belong to the same parametric measure and satisfy a strict conditional decomposition; the earlier apparent contradiction of "volumes differing by a hundred orders of magnitude while the hard function still competes" is not an SMC artifact.

3. Hard-to-margin continuous bridge

After the parent subset is advanced to L10<=0.065, all particles are hard-exact. At the tau=ln2 starting point, the four hard-cell branches reproduce the parent hard posterior with a TV of about 0.0086; tightening only the heldout margin, the absolute core masses of the four cells then separate continuously, with D440 ultimately gaining an overwhelming advantage. The separation occurs inside cells whose hard ID is unchanged, proving that the hard function is not the finest granularity of the complexity flow.

Individual branches stop advancing near deeper tau values; per the preregistration, only attainable lower or upper bounds are reported, without disguising them as zero volume.

4. Verdict

E20 establishes a static measurement closure:

  1. V_full and Omega_D are not the same quantity;
  2. they are strictly related through set inclusion and conditional probability;
  3. the full-rule volume can serve as a measurement with a bridging basis, rather than a post-hoc analogy;
  4. hard-function competition at fixed D and extremely deep full-rule volume differences can hold simultaneously;
  5. enormous volume contraction can still occur in the margin/continuous realization inside the same hard cell.

It does not prove that independently defined complex functions necessarily contract faster under all fixed D; the complexity direction still requires external criteria from the Neural K-profile and independent-sample data transition predictions.
